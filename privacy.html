1. Who is responsible for your information

The responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) is [Registered business name (Pty) Ltd], trading as Slick Velvet, registration number [reg. no.], of [Physical address, Cape Town].

Our Information Officer is [Name], reachable at [privacy@slickvelvet.co.za]. Contact them for anything in this policy.

2. What we collect

We collect only what we need to sell and deliver our products:

  • Order details — name, delivery address, email address, phone number, what you bought and when.
  • Payment — handled entirely by our payment provider over an encrypted connection. We receive confirmation that you paid and the last four digits of your card; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Account details if you create one — email, password (stored hashed), order history.
  • Messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data — IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, collected by our hosting provider and basic analytics to keep the Site secure and working.

We do not collect special personal information (such as health or sexual-life information) and we do not ask for it. Buying a product from us is not recorded as anything other than an order.

4. Discretion by design

Because of what we sell, we go further than the law requires:

  • Parcels are plain and unbranded; the sender shows as [neutral trading name] and the contents as "personal care".
  • Your bank or card statement shows [neutral merchant name], never "Slick Velvet" or the product.
  • Order confirmation emails have a neutral subject line and sender name.
  • We send no marketing unless you ask for it, and no post other than your parcel.
  • We never sell, rent or share customer lists with anyone.

5. Who we share it with

Only the operators we need to get your order to you, each bound by contract to protect your information and use it for that purpose alone:

  • Courier — [courier name]: name, address, phone number for delivery and tracking.
  • Payment provider — [PayFast / Yoco]: processes your payment under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and email — Vercel (website hosting) and [email provider] (order emails). Some of these providers store data outside South Africa, in countries with privacy laws that offer similar protection, as permitted by section 72 of POPIA.
  • Authorities — only where the law requires it.

6. Cookies and local storage

The Site uses a small amount of browser storage to remember that you confirmed you are over 18, your sound preference, and what is in your bag. These stay on your device and are not sent to us. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider records basic, aggregated visit statistics (page views, country) that do not identify you.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records: 5 years after the order, as required by South African tax law.
  • Account details: until you delete your account, or after 3 years of inactivity.
  • Messages and complaints: 2 years after they are resolved.
  • Technical logs: 90 days.

After that, information is deleted or anonymised.

8. How we protect it

The Site is served over HTTPS. Payments are processed by a PCI-DSS compliant provider. Access to order information is limited to the people who pack and support orders, and is password-protected. If a breach ever affects your information we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may, free of charge:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent to marketing at any time;
  • complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001, or via its online complaints portal.

To exercise a right, email our Information Officer at [privacy@slickvelvet.co.za]. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first. Our PAIA manual is available on request.

10. Children

The Site and our products are for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information we will email customers who have an account.
